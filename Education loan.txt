==================================================
              EDUCATION LOAN DETAILS
==================================================

1. BASIC INFORMATION
---------------------

Loan Type:
Education Loan

Purpose:
An education loan is a loan provided by a bank or
financial institution to help students pay for eligible
education-related expenses.

Common Purposes:
- Undergraduate education
- Postgraduate education
- Diploma courses
- Professional courses
- Technical courses
- Vocational courses
- Studies in India
- Studies abroad


2. STUDENT INFORMATION
-----------------------

Student ID:
Unique identification number of the student.

Student Name:
Full name of the student.

Date of Birth:
Date of birth of the student.

Age:
Age of the student.

Phone Number:
Registered phone number.

Email:
Email address of the student.

City:
City where the student lives.

Academic Qualification:
Student's current or previous qualification.

Academic Score:
Percentage, CGPA or other academic performance measure.


3. COURSE INFORMATION
---------------------

Course Name:
Name of the course being pursued.

Course Type:
- Undergraduate
- Postgraduate
- Diploma
- Professional
- Technical
- Vocational

College / University:
Name of the educational institution.

Country:
Country where the institution is located.

Course Duration:
Total duration of the course.

Admission Status:
- Applied
- Admission Confirmed
- Currently Studying


4. CO-APPLICANT INFORMATION
---------------------------

Co-Applicant Name:
Name of the parent, guardian or other eligible
co-applicant.

Relationship:
Relationship between student and co-applicant.

Co-Applicant Income:
Monthly or annual income of the co-applicant.

Co-Applicant Employment:
Employment or occupation of the co-applicant.

The lender may require a co-applicant depending on
the loan and applicant's circumstances.


5. EDUCATION EXPENSES
---------------------

Tuition Fees:
Fees charged by the educational institution.

Hostel Fees:
Cost of accommodation, if applicable.

Examination Fees:
Fees related to examinations.

Books and Equipment:
Cost of books, computers, equipment and other
eligible study materials.

Travel Expenses:
Eligible travel expenses, where applicable.

Other Expenses:
Other education-related expenses accepted by
the lender.


6. LOAN INFORMATION
-------------------

Loan Amount:
Amount borrowed to finance eligible education
expenses.

Loan Tenure:
Total period over which the loan is repaid.

Interest Rate:
Percentage of interest charged on the loan.

Interest Type:
- Fixed
- Floating

Moratorium Period:
A period during which repayment terms may differ,
often covering the course period and an additional
period specified by the lender.

Processing Fee:
Fee charged by the lender for processing the loan,
if applicable.


7. LOAN ELIGIBILITY
-------------------

Eligibility may depend on:

- Student's admission status
- Recognized educational institution
- Recognized course
- Academic record
- Age
- Co-applicant information
- Family income
- Credit history
- Required documents
- Loan amount
- Lender's eligibility criteria


8. CREDIT SCORE
---------------

Credit Score:
A numerical representation of a person's credit history.

The lender may consider the credit history of the
applicant and/or co-applicant while evaluating the
loan application.

Credit history may include:

- Previous loans
- Credit card payments
- Repayment history
- Outstanding debt
- Late payments
- Loan defaults


9. DOCUMENTS GENERALLY REQUIRED
-------------------------------

Student Documents:
- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Academic certificates
- Mark sheets
- Admission letter
- Course details
- Fee structure

Co-Applicant Documents:
- Identity proof
- Address proof
- Income documents
- Bank statements
- Employment documents

Other Documents:
- Loan application form
- Passport-size photographs
- Collateral documents, if required
- Other documents requested by the lender


10. EDUCATION LOAN PROCESS
--------------------------

Step 1:
Student selects an eligible course and institution.

Step 2:
Student receives admission confirmation.

Step 3:
Student submits the education-loan application.

Step 4:
Required student and co-applicant documents
are submitted.

Step 5:
The lender verifies the application and documents.

Step 6:
The lender evaluates eligibility and repayment
capacity.

Step 7:
If approved, the lender issues the loan sanction.

Step 8:
Required loan documentation is completed.

Step 9:
The loan amount is disbursed according to the
lender's terms and eligible expenses.

Step 10:
Repayment begins according to the agreed repayment
schedule.


11. LOAN STATUS
---------------

Possible loan statuses:

- Application Submitted
- Documents Pending
- Under Verification
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Sanctioned
- Disbursed
- Active
- Closed


12. EMI INFORMATION
-------------------

EMI:
Equated Monthly Instalment.

After the repayment period begins, the borrower may
repay the loan through monthly instalments.

EMI generally consists of:

- Principal
- Interest

EMI Formula:

EMI = P * R * (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N - 1)

Where:

P = Principal loan amount
R = Monthly interest rate
N = Number of monthly instalments


13. REPAYMENT
-------------

The borrower repays the education loan according to
the repayment schedule specified in the loan agreement.

Repayment may depend on:

- Loan amount
- Interest rate
- Loan tenure
- Moratorium arrangement
- Amount already paid
- Lender's repayment terms


14. PREPAYMENT
--------------

Part Prepayment:
The borrower pays a portion of the outstanding loan
before the scheduled repayment date.

Full Prepayment / Foreclosure:
The borrower pays the entire outstanding loan before
the original maturity date.

Applicable charges and conditions depend on the
loan agreement and lender.
