==================================================
              HOUSING LOAN DETAILS
==================================================

1. BASIC INFORMATION
---------------------

Loan Type:
Housing Loan / Home Loan

Purpose:
A housing loan is a loan provided by a bank or financial
institution to help a customer purchase, construct, or
renovate a residential property.

Common Purposes:
- Purchase of a new house
- Purchase of an existing house
- Construction of a house
- Home renovation
- Home extension


2. CUSTOMER INFORMATION
-----------------------

Account Holder Name:
The full name of the person applying for the loan.

Age:
Age of the applicant.

Phone Number:
Registered mobile number of the applicant.

Email:
Email address of the applicant.

City:
City where the applicant lives.

Employment Type:
- Salaried
- Self-employed
- Business owner

Monthly Income:
The applicant's monthly income.

Employment Experience:
Number of years the applicant has been working.


3. PROPERTY INFORMATION
------------------------

Property Type:
- Apartment
- Independent House
- Villa
- Residential Plot

Property Location:
Location of the property.

Property Value:
Total market/purchase value of the property.

Property Ownership:
Name of the person who owns the property.

Property Age:
Age of an existing property, if applicable.


4. LOAN INFORMATION
-------------------

Loan Amount:
Amount borrowed from the bank.

Down Payment:
Amount paid by the customer from their own funds.

Loan Tenure:
Duration for which the loan is taken.

Common Tenures:
- 5 years
- 10 years
- 15 years
- 20 years
- 25 years
- 30 years

Interest Rate:
Percentage of interest charged on the outstanding loan.

Interest Type:
- Fixed Interest Rate
- Floating Interest Rate


5. EMI INFORMATION
-------------------

EMI:
Equated Monthly Instalment.

EMI is the fixed monthly payment made by the borrower
towards the housing loan.

The EMI generally consists of:
- Principal component
- Interest component

EMI Formula:

EMI = P * R * (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N - 1)

Where:

P = Principal loan amount
R = Monthly interest rate
N = Number of monthly instalments


6. LOAN ELIGIBILITY
-------------------

Loan eligibility may depend on:

- Applicant's age
- Monthly income
- Employment type
- Employment stability
- Credit history
- Existing loans
- Monthly expenses
- Property value
- Loan tenure
- Bank's eligibility rules

The actual eligible amount is decided by the
lender after evaluating the applicant.


7. CREDIT SCORE
---------------

Credit Score:
A numerical indicator used by lenders to assess
a borrower's credit history.

A stronger credit history may help an applicant
qualify for better loan terms, subject to the
lender's policies.

Credit history may include:
- Previous loans
- Credit card payments
- Repayment history
- Outstanding debts
- Defaults or late payments


8. DOCUMENTS GENERALLY REQUIRED
-------------------------------

Common documents may include:

Identity Proof:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Driving Licence

Address Proof:
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Utility Bill
- Other accepted address documents

Income Documents:
For salaried applicants:
- Salary slips
- Bank statements
- Form 16 / income-tax documents

For self-employed applicants:
- Income-tax returns
- Business financial statements
- Bank statements

Property Documents:
- Sale agreement
- Property title documents
- Approved building plan
- Property tax documents
- Other documents requested by the lender


9. LOAN PROCESS
---------------

Step 1:
Customer submits the housing loan application.

Step 2:
Bank verifies the applicant's identity and documents.

Step 3:
Bank evaluates income, employment, credit history,
existing liabilities and other eligibility factors.

Step 4:
Bank evaluates the property and its documents.

Step 5:
If approved, the bank issues a loan sanction.

Step 6:
Customer completes the required agreement and
documentation.

Step 7:
The loan is disbursed according to the lender's
terms and the stage of the property transaction.

Step 8:
Customer pays the EMI according to the repayment
schedule.


10. LOAN STATUS
---------------

Possible loan statuses:

- Application Submitted
- Under Verification
- Documents Pending
- Property Verification
- Approved
- Rejected
- Sanctioned
- Disbursed
- Active
- Closed


11. REPAYMENT
-------------

The borrower normally repays the loan through
monthly EMI payments.

Each EMI generally contains:

Principal:
The portion used to reduce the outstanding loan.

Interest:
The amount charged by the lender for providing
the loan.

As the outstanding principal decreases, the interest
portion of the EMI generally decreases and the
principal portion generally increases, depending on
the repayment structure.


12. PREPAYMENT
-------------

Prepayment means paying part or all of the outstanding
loan before the scheduled maturity date.

Types:

Part Prepayment:
A portion of the outstanding principal is paid early.

Full Prepayment / Foreclosure:
The entire outstanding loan is paid before maturity.

Applicable charges and conditions depend on the
loan agreement and lender.
