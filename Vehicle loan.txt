==================================================
              VEHICLE LOAN DETAILS
==================================================

1. BASIC INFORMATION
---------------------

Loan Type:
Vehicle Loan

Purpose:
A vehicle loan is a loan provided by a bank or financial
institution to help a customer purchase a vehicle.

Common Purposes:
- Purchase of a new car
- Purchase of a used car
- Purchase of a two-wheeler
- Purchase of a commercial vehicle


2. CUSTOMER INFORMATION
-----------------------

Customer ID:
Unique identification number of the customer.

Account Holder Name:
Full name of the loan applicant.

Age:
Age of the applicant.

Phone Number:
Registered mobile number.

Email:
Email address of the applicant.

City:
City where the applicant lives.

Employment Type:
- Salaried
- Self-employed
- Business owner

Monthly Income:
Monthly income of the applicant.

Employment Experience:
Number of years the applicant has been employed.


3. VEHICLE INFORMATION
----------------------

Vehicle Type:
- Car
- Bike
- Scooter
- Commercial Vehicle

Vehicle Brand:
Brand or manufacturer of the vehicle.

Vehicle Model:
Model of the vehicle.

Vehicle Year:
Manufacturing or registration year.

New or Used:
- New
- Used

Vehicle Price:
Total purchase price of the vehicle.

Dealer Name:
Name of the vehicle dealer.


4. LOAN INFORMATION
-------------------

Loan Amount:
Amount borrowed from the bank or financial institution.

Down Payment:
Amount paid by the customer towards the vehicle purchase.

Loan Tenure:
Duration for which the loan is taken.

Common Tenures:
- 1 year
- 2 years
- 3 years
- 4 years
- 5 years
- 7 years

Interest Rate:
Percentage of interest charged on the loan.

Interest Type:
- Fixed
- Floating


5. EMI INFORMATION
-------------------

EMI:
Equated Monthly Instalment.

The borrower normally pays the EMI every month until
the loan is completely repaid.

EMI consists mainly of:
- Principal
- Interest

EMI Formula:

EMI = P * R * (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N - 1)

Where:

P = Loan principal
R = Monthly interest rate
N = Number of monthly instalments


6. LOAN ELIGIBILITY
-------------------

Eligibility may depend on:

- Applicant's age
- Monthly income
- Employment type
- Employment stability
- Credit history
- Existing loans
- Existing EMI obligations
- Vehicle price
- Loan amount
- Loan tenure
- Lender's eligibility criteria


7. CREDIT SCORE
---------------

Credit Score:
A numerical representation of a person's credit history.

Lenders may consider credit history when evaluating
a vehicle-loan application.

Credit history may include:
- Previous loans
- Credit card payments
- Repayment history
- Outstanding debt
- Late payments
- Loan defaults


8. DOCUMENTS GENERALLY REQUIRED
-------------------------------

Identity Proof:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Driving Licence

Address Proof:
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Utility Bill
- Other accepted documents

Income Documents:
For salaried applicants:
- Salary slips
- Bank statements
- Form 16
- Income-tax documents

For self-employed applicants:
- Income-tax returns
- Business documents
- Bank statements
- Financial statements

Vehicle Documents:
- Vehicle quotation
- Invoice
- Registration documents
- Insurance documents
- Other documents requested by the lender


9. VEHICLE LOAN PROCESS
-----------------------

Step 1:
Customer selects a vehicle.

Step 2:
Customer submits the vehicle-loan application.

Step 3:
Bank verifies the customer's identity and documents.

Step 4:
Bank evaluates income, credit history and existing
financial obligations.

Step 5:
Bank determines loan eligibility.

Step 6:
If approved, the bank issues the loan sanction.

Step 7:
Required loan documentation is completed.

Step 8:
The loan amount is disbursed according to the
lender's terms.

Step 9:
Customer starts paying monthly EMI.


10. LOAN STATUS
---------------

Possible loan statuses:

- Application Submitted
- Under Verification
- Documents Pending
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Sanctioned
- Disbursed
- Active
- Closed


11. REPAYMENT
-------------

The customer repays the vehicle loan through monthly
EMI payments.

Each EMI generally contains:

Principal:
Amount used to reduce the outstanding loan balance.

Interest:
Amount charged by the lender for providing the loan.

The outstanding principal generally decreases as
the borrower makes repayments.


12. PREPAYMENT
--------------

Part Prepayment:
The borrower pays a portion of the outstanding loan
before the scheduled repayment date.

Full Prepayment / Foreclosure:
The borrower pays the entire outstanding loan before
the original maturity date.

Any applicable charges and conditions depend on the
loan agreement and lender.
