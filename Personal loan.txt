==================================================
              PERSONAL LOAN DETAILS
==================================================

1. BASIC INFORMATION
---------------------

Loan Type:
Personal Loan

Purpose:
A personal loan is generally an unsecured loan that can
be used for various personal expenses.

Common Purposes:
- Medical expenses
- Education expenses
- Wedding expenses
- Travel expenses
- Home renovation
- Emergency expenses
- Debt consolidation
- Consumer purchases
- Other personal expenses


2. CUSTOMER INFORMATION
-----------------------

Customer ID:
Unique identification number of the customer.

Account Holder Name:
Full name of the loan applicant.

Age:
Age of the applicant.

Phone Number:
Registered mobile number.

Email:
Email address of the applicant.

City:
City where the applicant lives.

Employment Type:
- Salaried
- Self-employed
- Business owner

Monthly Income:
Monthly income of the applicant.

Employment Experience:
Number of years the applicant has been employed.


3. LOAN INFORMATION
-------------------

Loan Amount:
Amount borrowed from the bank or financial institution.

Loan Purpose:
Reason for taking the personal loan.

Loan Tenure:
Duration for which the loan is taken.

Common Tenures:
- 1 year
- 2 years
- 3 years
- 4 years
- 5 years
- 7 years

Interest Rate:
Percentage of interest charged on the loan.

Interest Type:
- Fixed
- Floating

Processing Fee:
Fee charged by the lender for processing the loan
application, if applicable.


4. EMI INFORMATION
-------------------

EMI:
Equated Monthly Instalment.

The borrower normally pays an EMI every month until
the loan is completely repaid.

EMI generally consists of:
- Principal
- Interest

EMI Formula:

EMI = P * R * (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N - 1)

Where:

P = Principal loan amount
R = Monthly interest rate
N = Number of monthly instalments


5. LOAN ELIGIBILITY
-------------------

Eligibility may depend on:

- Applicant's age
- Monthly income
- Employment type
- Employment stability
- Credit history
- Credit score
- Existing loans
- Existing EMI obligations
- Loan amount
- Loan tenure
- Lender's eligibility criteria


6. CREDIT SCORE
---------------

Credit Score:
A numerical representation of a person's credit history.

Lenders may consider credit history while evaluating
a personal-loan application.

Credit history may include:

- Previous loans
- Credit card payments
- Repayment history
- Outstanding debt
- Late payments
- Loan defaults


7. DOCUMENTS GENERALLY REQUIRED
-------------------------------

Identity Proof:
- Aadhaar Card
- PAN Card
- Passport
- Driving Licence

Address Proof:
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Utility Bill
- Other accepted documents

Income Documents:

For salaried applicants:
- Salary slips
- Bank statements
- Form 16
- Income-tax documents

For self-employed applicants:
- Income-tax returns
- Business documents
- Bank statements
- Financial statements

Additional Documents:
- Loan application form
- Passport-size photographs
- Other documents requested by the lender


8. PERSONAL LOAN PROCESS
------------------------

Step 1:
Customer submits the personal-loan application.

Step 2:
Customer provides the required documents.

Step 3:
Bank verifies the customer's identity and documents.

Step 4:
Bank evaluates income, employment, credit history
and existing financial obligations.

Step 5:
Bank determines the customer's eligibility.

Step 6:
If approved, the bank issues the loan sanction.

Step 7:
Required loan documentation is completed.

Step 8:
The approved loan amount is disbursed according
to the lender's terms.

Step 9:
Customer starts paying monthly EMI.


9. LOAN STATUS
--------------

Possible loan statuses:

- Application Submitted
- Under Verification
- Documents Pending
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Sanctioned
- Disbursed
- Active
- Closed


10. REPAYMENT
-------------

The borrower repays the personal loan through monthly
EMI payments.

Each EMI generally contains:

Principal:
Amount used to reduce the outstanding loan balance.

Interest:
Amount charged by the lender for providing the loan.

As the loan is repaid, the outstanding principal
generally decreases.


11. PREPAYMENT
--------------

Part Prepayment:
The borrower pays a portion of the outstanding loan
before the scheduled repayment date.

Full Prepayment / Foreclosure:
The borrower pays the entire outstanding loan before
the original maturity date.

Applicable charges and conditions depend on the
loan agreement and lender.
